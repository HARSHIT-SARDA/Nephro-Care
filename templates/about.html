Our purpose

What NephroCare aims to do

A simple academic demonstration connecting a web interface, a Python backend, a database and a trained ML model.

Structured screening

Collect selected clinical values through a guided assessment form.

ML-assisted estimate

Use the configured Random Forest model to generate an educational risk estimate from the submitted values.

History and reports

Allow signed-in users to review saved assessments and access available reports.

Important health notice

NephroCare is an academic screening prototype, not a medical device or diagnostic service. Its result cannot confirm or rule out CKD. Please discuss symptoms and laboratory results with a qualified healthcare professional.
